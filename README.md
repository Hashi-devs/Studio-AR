# Studio-AR

Sketch Studio by AR Studios: turn a photo of a drawing into clean digital art, right in the browser.

- **Type:** Line art (one ink colour), Coloured (keeps the colours), or Photo.
- **Clean-up** and **Lines** sliders, and a switch to remove notebook lines (ruled lines and the margin line).
- **Erase & crop** editor: brush eraser with undo, crop box, zoom and pan.
- Preview on paper textures and frames, then download PNG / SVG or copy for Figma.

Everything runs on the device; nothing is uploaded. The same tool lives at art-studio.ar-studio.workers.dev/sketch-studio.

Fonts: IM Fell English (Google Fonts) and Google Sans Flex (SIL Open Font License, in `fonts/`).
